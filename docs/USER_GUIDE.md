# User guide

This guide is also available inside the platform: the **Help** centre (`/help`), the **?** button on every page, and the guided tour that starts after your first sign-in (you can restart it from Help).

> In a demo deployment a banner says so on every page. Demo balances have no value and nothing is sent on a real blockchain.

## 1. Create your account

1. **Register** with your email, a password and your country. The password needs 12+ characters with upper- and lower-case letters, a number and a symbol; the form shows which rules are met as you type.
2. Accept the terms, the privacy policy and the risk disclosure.
3. Open the confirmation email and click the link. You land on your dashboard.
4. If someone invited you, open their link or type their code at registration — it cannot be added later.

## 2. Turn on two-factor sign-in

**Security → Two-factor authentication.** Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy …), type the 6-digit code. From then on, sign-in asks for a code. Two-factor sign-in is required to add withdrawal addresses and to withdraw.

## 3. Your dashboard

- **Total balance** and the **ledger strip** — one bar showing where your money is:
  - *Available* — free to invest or withdraw,
  - *Deposit hold* — recent deposits, withdrawable after 7 days (you can invest them right away),
  - *Withdrawal hold* — reserved by withdrawals you requested,
  - *Invested* — in investment positions.
- Recent activity, open positions, pending deposits and withdrawals.
- The dot in the top bar shows the live connection. Green: updates arrive by themselves. Amber: reconnecting — the page refreshes when the connection is back.

## 4. Deposit

1. **Deposit** shows our USDT address on **BEP20 (BNB Smart Chain)** with a QR code and a copy button. Send only USDT on BEP20 — other coins or networks are lost.
2. After sending, press **I have sent it** with the amount and, if you have them, the transaction ID and the wallet you sent from. The payment is linked to your account, so we know it is yours.
3. The deposit shows *Being checked*. Our team finds it on the blockchain and credits the amount that arrived; you get a notification. Your balance does not change before that.
4. Credited funds are on a **7-day hold** for withdrawals. You can invest them immediately.

## 5. Invest

1. **Investments → Products.** Each product shows its term (for example 28 days), minimum and maximum, the performance fee, whether early exit is allowed and its fee, the risk level, how returns are generated and the full risk disclosure.
2. The calculator lets you try a *hypothetical* rate — it is a scenario, not a promise.
3. **Open position** → enter the amount → confirm. The amount moves from Available to Invested.
4. Results are added **only when realised**: after each period the strategy's actual result (profit or loss) is recorded, checked by a second person and then shared out to the positions that were open. You see each result on the position and in **Earnings**.
5. At the end of the term the position's value returns to Available automatically. If the last period's result is not posted yet, the position shows *Awaiting settlement* — nothing is estimated.
6. **Close early** (only if the product allows it) returns the current value minus the early-exit fee shown before you confirm.

Investments can lose value. Past results do not predict future results.

## 6. Withdraw

1. **Withdrawals** → paste your USDT address on **BEP20** (it starts with `0x`) and enter the amount. You can withdraw your *withdrawable* balance (available minus funds on deposit hold). Two-factor sign-in is needed.
2. **Continue** shows the exact quote from the server: platform fee **3%**, network fee (1 USDT on BEP20) and what arrives. Example: 100 USDT → 3.00 + 1.00 in fees → 96.00 USDT arrives. A quote is valid for 5 minutes.
3. **Confirm withdrawal.** The amount is reserved and the request goes to our team. They send it to your address and mark it as paid; you get a notification and an email with the transaction. You can cancel while it is *Pending*; if it is rejected, the funds return to Available.

## 7. Transaction history

**Transactions** lists every movement — deposits, withdrawals, fees, investment allocations, results, releases, referral rewards — with filters by type, asset, status and date. Click a row for details (reference, blockchain link, fees). **Export CSV** downloads the filtered list.

## 8. Invite friends

**Referrals** shows your code and link. You receive:

- **5%** of each deposit your invited friends make (after it is credited),
- **2%** of the profit they actually realise.

Rewards are paid by the platform — never taken from your friend's money — and become available after a 7-day hold.

## 9. Notifications, profile, support

- **Notifications** — deposits, withdrawals, results, rewards, security alerts. The bell shows unread ones.
- **Profile** — display name, language, theme (light, dark or system), identity verification status.
- **Security** — password, two-factor, recent sign-ins and security events. Changing your password signs out your other devices.
- **Support** — open a ticket and follow the conversation with our team.

## Languages and accessibility

English, Russian, Arabic (right-to-left) and Georgian. Switch in the top bar; your choice is saved to your profile. The site works with a keyboard and screen readers, respects your system's reduced-motion setting and works on phones.
