Risk disclosure

Please read this before you deposit or invest. It describes the main risks; it is not a complete list.

  • You can lose money

    Investment results can be negative. A loss is posted to your position exactly like a gain. You may get back less than you invested.

  • No guaranteed returns

    We do not promise any return. Past results, including those shown on product pages, do not predict future results.

  • Crypto prices are volatile

    The value of crypto assets can change quickly and by large amounts. Values shown in US dollars are approximate and can be out of date.

  • Your money is not always immediately available

    New deposits cannot be withdrawn for 7 days. Invested funds are committed for the product term; early exit, where allowed, has a fee. Withdrawals are reviewed and can take time, and may be paused if a reconciliation finds a problem.

  • Counterparty and operational risk

    Strategies may use exchanges, lending venues or custodians that can fail, be hacked or freeze withdrawals. Our own systems can also fail despite the controls we use.

  • Blockchain risks

    Funds sent on the wrong network, without a required memo or to a wrong address may be lost permanently. Blockchain transactions cannot be reversed.

  • Legal and regulatory risk

    Rules for crypto assets differ by country and change. A product may have to be withdrawn, and you are responsible for tax on your results where you live.

  • No deposit insurance

    Crypto assets held with us are not covered by bank deposit guarantee schemes or investor compensation schemes unless a product page says otherwise.

This disclosure is provided in English, Russian and Arabic. If the translations differ, the English version prevails.
