What we use

We use only cookies that are needed for the service to work. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Session cookies

Secure, HTTP-only cookies keep you signed in. Scripts on the page cannot read them, and they are sent only to our own site.

Security cookie

A separate cookie protects forms against cross-site request forgery.

Preferences on your device

Your chosen language and theme are saved in your browser’s local storage. No financial data or sign-in tokens are stored there.

Your choices

You can delete cookies in your browser settings. If you delete the session cookies you will be signed out.

This document is available in English, Russian and Arabic. If the versions differ, the English version prevails.
