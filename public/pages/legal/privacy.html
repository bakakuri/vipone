Who is responsible for your data

The operator identified in the published terms is the controller of your personal data.

What we collect

Account data (email, country, language), verification data provided through our identity-verification provider, transaction data (deposits, withdrawals, positions, ledger entries), security data (sign-in events, device information and a one-way hash of your IP address) and your messages to support.

Why we use it

To provide the account and its services, to meet legal obligations such as anti-money-laundering checks and record keeping, to protect you and the platform against fraud, and to communicate with you about your account.

Who we share it with

Service providers acting on our instructions — hosting and database, email delivery, identity verification, blockchain data and screening — and authorities where the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Financial and verification records are kept for the period required by law, which can be several years after your account closes. Other data is deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask for access to, correction of, deletion of or a copy of your data, and object to or restrict some uses. Records we must keep by law cannot be deleted before the end of the legal period.

Security

Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and never stored in readable form. Sessions use secure cookies that scripts cannot read. Access by staff is limited by role and recorded in an audit log.

Contact

Send privacy requests through Support in your account or to the address given in the published version of this policy.

This document is available in English, Russian and Arabic. If the versions differ, the English version prevails.
