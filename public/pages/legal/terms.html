Who we are and what these terms cover

These terms govern your use of the Ledgerline platform and your account. The operator of the platform is identified in the published version of these terms, together with its registration number and supervising authority where one applies.

You must be at least 18 years old, have full legal capacity and not be resident in a country where we do not offer the service. We may ask you to verify your identity and the source of your funds, and we may refuse or close an account where the law requires it.

Your account and security

Keep your password and two-factor device safe. We will never ask for them. You are responsible for activity on your account until you tell us it has been compromised; contact support immediately if you suspect it has been.

Deposits

Deposits are credited after the number of blockchain confirmations shown on the deposit page. Funds sent on an unsupported network, without a required memo or to an address that is not yours may not be recoverable. A credited deposit cannot be withdrawn for 7 days.

Investment products

Each product states what generates its return, its term, fees, limits and risks. When you invest, the version of the product terms in force at that moment applies to your position. Results are posted only after they are realised and approved; they can be negative.

Withdrawals and fees

Withdrawals are sent only to addresses you have added and confirmed. Before you confirm a withdrawal you are shown the platform fee, the network fee and the amount that will arrive. Requests are reviewed and may be delayed or refused where the law or our risk controls require it.

Referral programme

Referral rewards are paid by the operator from its own funds and are subject to the programme rules shown in your account. Rewards obtained through self-referral, multiple accounts or other abuse are cancelled.

Prohibited use

You may not use the platform for money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions evasion, fraud or any other unlawful purpose, or attempt to interfere with its security.

Liability

Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by law. Subject to that, the operator is not liable for losses caused by market movements, by blockchain networks or by events outside its reasonable control.

Changes and termination

We will give you notice of material changes to these terms before they take effect. You can close your account at any time after withdrawing your available balance; open positions remain subject to their terms until they are released.

Governing law and complaints
