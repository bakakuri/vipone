Frequently asked questions

Short answers. The help centre has step-by-step guides.

What is Ledgerline?

An account for depositing crypto, investing it in products with published terms and withdrawing it, with every balance kept on a double-entry ledger you can inspect.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Results can be positive or negative, and only realised results are added to your position. No page shows a projected return.

Why can’t I withdraw a new deposit straight away?

For 7 days after a deposit is credited it can be invested but not withdrawn. This protects all customers against payment fraud and money laundering. Your dashboard shows when each held amount is released.

What does the 28-day term mean?

Money invested in a 28-day product stays in that position for 28 days. The progress bar shows elapsed time only. At maturity the position is released to your available balance once the result for its last period is posted.

What fees do you charge?

Withdrawals: a 3% platform fee plus the blockchain network fee, both shown before you confirm. Products may charge a performance fee on gains and, where early exit is allowed, an early-exit fee. Deposits are free.

How long does a withdrawal take?

Each request is reviewed by staff before it is sent, and larger amounts need a second approver, so timing depends on that review. You can follow every step on the withdrawals page and you get an email when the funds are sent.

How does the referral programme work?

You get 5% of each confirmed deposit made by people you invite and 2% of their realised, posted profit. Rewards are paid by the platform, not taken from the person you invited, and they stay pending for a short check before they are credited.

I sent funds on the wrong network. What now?

Contact support with the transaction hash. Funds sent on an unsupported network or without a required memo may not be recoverable; when they can be, recovery requires a manual review.

Will you ever ask for my seed phrase?

Never. We store only public withdrawal addresses. Nobody from Ledgerline will ask for your password, two-factor codes, private keys or seed phrase.

Do I need to verify my identity?

Identity verification may be required before you can get a deposit address, invest or withdraw, depending on your country and the law that applies to us.

What is demo mode?

A demo deployment shows a banner on every page. Its balances and transactions are test data and have no value.
