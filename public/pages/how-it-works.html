How Ledgerline works

What happens to your money at each step, which rules apply and what you can check yourself.

The ledger

Every balance you see is calculated from ledger entries. Each operation is recorded as a transaction whose debits and credits are equal, and recorded entries cannot be edited or deleted — a correction is a new, separately approved entry.

In your transaction history you can open any line and see the entries behind it.

Deposits

You get a personal address for each network. Send only the asset and network shown on the deposit page; some networks also need a memo or tag. A deposit is credited after the required number of blockchain confirmations.

Deposits that fail an automated risk check are held for review by our compliance staff before anything is credited.

The 7-day rule

A credited deposit cannot be withdrawn for 7 days. During that time you can invest it. Your dashboard shows the held amount separately and the date it is released.

Investing and the 28-day term

Each product describes what generates its return, its term, fees, limits and risks. When you invest, the amount moves from available to invested and the product terms you accepted are stored with your position.

The term progress bar shows elapsed time only. It is not a projection of profit.

How results are posted

After a period ends, our finance team records the realised result with evidence, such as an exchange or custodian statement. A second staff member must approve it before it is posted. Results are allocated to every position that was open during the period in proportion to the time it was open.

A performance fee is charged only on gains. Losses are posted in exactly the same way as gains. If a term ends before the result for its last period is posted, the position shows "awaiting settlement" until it is.

Withdrawals and fees

Withdrawals go only to addresses you have added and confirmed by email; a new address can be used after a short cooldown. Before you confirm, you see a quote with the platform fee (3%), the network fee and the exact amount that will arrive.

Worked example

You withdraw
100 USDT
Platform fee, 3%
− 3 USDT
Network fee, as quoted
− 1.5 USDT
Arrives at your address
95.5 USDT

The network fee differs by network and is shown in your quote.

Every request is reviewed by staff before it is sent. Requests above a set amount need approval from two different people, and the person who sends the funds cannot be the one who confirms the payment arrived.

Referral programme

When someone you invite makes a confirmed deposit, you receive 5% of that deposit. When they earn a realised, posted profit, you receive 2% of that profit. Both rewards are paid by the platform from its own revenue; nothing is deducted from the person you invited.

Rewards stay pending for a short period and are checked for abuse, such as self-referral, before they are credited.

Security

Sign-in sessions use secure cookies that scripts cannot read. Two-factor authentication is required to add withdrawal addresses and to withdraw. We never ask for your password, codes, private keys or seed phrase — not by email, chat or phone.
