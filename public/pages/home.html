From deposit to withdrawal

The same five steps for every account, with the rules written down before you start.

  1. Blockchain confirmations

    Deposit

    Send crypto to your personal address. It is credited after the network confirms it, never before.

  2. 7 days

    Hold

    A new deposit cannot be withdrawn for 7 days. You can invest it straight away.

  3. 28-day term

    Invest

    Choose a product, read what generates its return and its risks, and accept the terms in force that day.

  4. Realised only

    Results

    A result is posted after the period has closed and a second staff member has checked the evidence. Losses are posted the same way.

  5. 3% + network fee

    Withdraw

    You see the exact fee and the amount that arrives before you confirm. Staff review each request before it is sent.

Rules we hold ourselves to

Written into the database, not just this page: the system refuses to break them.

Always

  • Every movement of money is a balanced debit and credit you can inspect.
  • Large withdrawals and every relaxation of a control need two different people.
  • Platform assets are reconciled against user balances, and withdrawals pause if they do not match.
  • Referral rewards are paid from the platform's own revenue.

Never

  • Promise or display a return that has not been earned.
  • Pay one customer's returns with another customer's deposit.
  • Ask for your private keys, seed phrase or passwords.
  • Change a balance without a recorded, approved reason.

Products

Each product shows what generates its return, its fees and its realised history. None of them shows a projected return.

Before you invest

The value of crypto assets can fall sharply and you can lose the money you invest. Past results do not predict future ones. Only invest what you can afford to lose, and read the risk disclosure first.
